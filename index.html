Installing CoreRetail

Pick your device. About two minutes either way.

CoreRetail installs directly — no Play Store or Microsoft Store account needed on your end or ours.

01

Download the APK

Tap the download button below on the Android phone you'll use for sales.

02

Allow the install

Your phone will ask to allow installs from this source — tap Settings, then turn it on.

03

Open the file

Go back to your downloads, tap the file, then tap Install.

04

Sign in & sell

Open CoreRetail, sign in, and you're ready to ring up your first sale.
